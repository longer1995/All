# Deluxe — E-commerce Transaction Economics

> **Context:** Deluxe asked for the average transaction size and average monthly transaction
> count for the e-commerce side of the business. The only e-commerce operation in Brandon's
> profile is **Ammunation LLC** (2020 – Apr 2026, exited), whose multi-channel GTM included
> an e-commerce channel alongside wholesale, retail, and direct accounts.
>
> ⚠️ **The exact channel-level numbers are not in this workspace.** What follows is (1) the
> hard facts we do have, (2) industry benchmarks, (3) defensible scenario estimates, and
> (4) the exact records Brandon should pull to replace the estimates with real figures.
> Do not present the scenario numbers to Deluxe as actuals.

## 1. Known facts (from the resume baseline)

- Ammunation grew to **~$4.8M annual revenue across 75+ accounts**
- Revenue was **multi-channel**: wholesale, retail, e-commerce, and direct accounts
- The Nobel Sport (Browning) exclusive carried a **$3M annual minimum** — that volume is
  distribution/wholesale, not e-commerce
- CRM/commerce stack included **Zoho and ReadyCloud** (ReadyCloud is where the order-level
  e-commerce data will live)

Implication: e-commerce was a **minority channel**. With $3M+ of the $4.8M anchored in the
wholesale exclusive, the realistic e-commerce band is roughly **$0.5M–$1.5M/year**.

## 2. Industry benchmarks (online ammunition retail)

- ammo.com: AOV **$175–$200** (May 2025, Grips Intelligence)
- sgammo.com: AOV **$300–$325** (June 2026, Grips Intelligence) — high end of the niche
- General US e-commerce AOV: **~$145–$155** — ammo retail runs well above it

A **$175–$300 average ticket** is the defensible range for the category; **~$200–$250**
is a safe midpoint to quote if pressed before real data is pulled.

## 3. Scenario estimates (monthly transaction count)

Monthly transactions = (annual e-comm revenue ÷ AOV) ÷ 12

| E-comm annual revenue | AOV $175 | AOV $225 | AOV $300 |
|---|---|---|---|
| $500K | ~240/mo | ~185/mo | ~140/mo |
| $1.0M | ~475/mo | ~370/mo | ~280/mo |
| $1.5M | ~715/mo | ~555/mo | ~415/mo |

Headline framing if an order-of-magnitude answer is acceptable:
**"average ticket in the low-to-mid hundreds ($200–$250), a few hundred e-commerce
transactions per month, on roughly $0.5–1.5M of annual online revenue within a $4.8M
multi-channel business."**

## 4. What Brandon needs to pull for exact numbers

1. **ReadyCloud / storefront reports**: total e-commerce orders and gross e-commerce
   revenue for the trailing 12 months of operation → AOV = revenue ÷ orders;
   monthly count = orders ÷ 12
2. **Payment processor statements** (whoever processed card payments): monthly settlement
   summaries state average ticket and transaction count directly — this is exactly the
   format a payments company like Deluxe expects
3. Note any **seasonality** (hunting / sporting-clays seasons drove cyclical demand) —
   quote both the average month and the peak month
