# Auto-Apply Setup Guide — JobCopilot

**Goal:** set it up once (~15 min), then it applies in the background while you're with your kids / building your company. Uses your accurate profile so it doesn't auto-submit fake details.

> Works almost identically for **LoopCV** if you'd rather start on a free tier. Steps are the same idea; menu names differ slightly.

---

## Before you start — have these open
- [[Auto-Apply Master Profile]] (all your fields, filled accurately)
- Your best resume PDF (see "Which resume" below)
- 30 seconds to finish the 4 legal/logistics answers if not done

## Step 1 — Create the account
1. Go to **jobcopilot.com** → sign up.
2. Pick a plan by how much volume you want:
   - **Premium** (~20 apps/day) is plenty for targeted volume.
   - **Elite** (~50/day) only if you want to cast very wide.

## Step 2 — Paste your profile (accuracy matters most here)
Copy each field from [[Auto-Apply Master Profile]] into JobCopilot's profile setup. **Do not skip these — they're the ones that protect you:**
- **Education → "Some college, no degree." Bachelor's = No.** (Never let it flip this to a degree.)
- **Work history** exactly as listed (titles, companies, dates). Leave **GCDL Defense** spelled as-is.
- **Work auth / sponsorship / relocation / start date** → your 4 answers (start date = Immediately ✅).
- **Salary:** enter a *range* starting realistically, not a hard $140K floor (a high floor auto-filters you out).

## Step 3 — Upload the right resume
- **Default:** upload your **Sales Track** resume PDF ([[Sales Track Resume]]).
- Let JobCopilot's AI tailor per job **on top of** your real resume — because it's starting from your accurate base, its tailoring stays truthful instead of inventing a generic one.
- ⚠️ After setup, **spot-check the first 3 resumes it generates** for the degree line and company names before trusting it to run unattended. Five minutes now saves you from a wrong detail on 200 applications.

## Step 4 — Set filters so it stays TARGETED (this is the anti-spam dial)
This is what separates "targeted apply" from "spray 500." Set:
- **Titles:** Account Executive, Enterprise Account Executive, Mid-Market Account Executive, Sales Executive, Business Development
- **Location:** Remote (US) + Texas (Houston, Austin)
- **Seniority:** Mid–Senior (skip entry-level SDR unless you want the volume)
- **Exclude keywords:** "SDR" / "intern" / "part-time" if you don't want those
- **Daily cap:** start at **15–20/day.** Enough volume, still targeted. You can raise it later.

## Step 5 — Turn it on, then check back
- Enable auto-apply.
- **Day 1–2:** review what it submitted (JobCopilot logs every application). Confirm the resumes were accurate and the jobs were real fits. Tune filters if it's drifting off-target.
- After that it runs on its own. Check the log a couple times a week.

## Step 6 — The high-value 15 minutes/week that beats the bot
For your **top 5–10 fits each week** (from [[Tier 2 - Good Pay]] / [[Tier 3 - Long Shots]]), send a short LinkedIn message to the VP of Sales / hiring manager. Outbound to a decision-maker is literally your job — it converts far better than any auto-application, and it's where you'll actually land the good roles. Let the bot handle the wide net; you handle the few that matter.

---

## Honest expectations
- Auto-apply callback rates run ~1–6%. Tailored + direct-outreach roles run ~9–15%. So: **let the bot cover volume, but put your limited time into the top handful directly.** That combination is how your friend's approach works *and* how you land the roles worth having.
- Keep the daily cap sane. Employers flag obvious mass-generic applications; a targeted 15–20/day with your accurate tailored resume does not look like that.
